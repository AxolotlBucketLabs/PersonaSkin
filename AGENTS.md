## AxolotlBucketLabs agent guidance

For this Axolotl-PM plugin, follow the [AxolotlBucketLabs skill](https://github.com/AxolotlBucketLabs/axolotl-bucket-labs/blob/main/SKILL.md). Read its [writing style](https://github.com/AxolotlBucketLabs/axolotl-bucket-labs/blob/main/references/writing-style.md) before editing human-facing text.

Write naturally and concisely. Do not use em dash or en dash punctuation or semicolons in prose. Avoid unnecessary `Label: content` phrasing. Use Markdown inline code for technical names and paths.
